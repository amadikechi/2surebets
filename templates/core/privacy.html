Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

1. What we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and a hashed password.
  • Subscription data: plan chosen, start and expiry dates, payment status.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited — used to keep the site secure and working.

2. How we use it

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To grant or revoke access to premium picks.
  • To process payments and respond to support requests.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

3. Payment data

We do not store card numbers or CVV codes. Card data is handled entirely by our payment provider (e.g. Paystack). We only store a payment reference and the amount paid so we can match payments to subscriptions.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to run the site (hosting, email, payments) and only to the extent required for those services.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies only — to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account by emailing {{ site_support_email }}. Deletion removes your account and any associated personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain records for legal or accounting purposes.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed using Django's built-in secure hashing. Payments are processed over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure — please choose a strong, unique password.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on the site.

9. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to {{ site_support_email }}.
