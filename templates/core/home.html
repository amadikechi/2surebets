Questions

Common questions

Are the picks guaranteed to win?

No. Nobody can guarantee a betting outcome, and anyone who claims otherwise is not being honest with you. Our picks are selections we would place ourselves — nothing more. You should only bet what you can afford to lose.

How do I receive the codes?

Once subscribed, log in and open today's pick. The full betting code is displayed for you and can be copied with a single click. Codes are retrieved from our server — they are never sent to non-subscribers' browsers.

What sports and leagues do you cover?

Mostly football, across a range of leagues. Occasionally we include other sports when a strong selection appears. Each pick shows its description before you unlock it, so you always know what you're getting.

Can I see the results before subscribing?

Yes. The results page is public and unfiltered. It shows wins, losses, and pending picks in full. We would rather earn your trust with a real record than a curated one.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions run for their chosen period and auto renew but you can cancel anytime.

Is this available outside Nigeria?

Yes, though payment options and legal requirements vary by country. You're responsible for ensuring betting is legal where you live before subscribing.
