About

Who's behind {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} is a small, independent operation. We publish one or two hand-selected betting picks per day, and nothing else. No filler, auto-generated lists, no paywalled "premium VIP" upsells.

How picks are chosen

Every selection is made by hand. We look at fixtures, form, injuries, and odds across a small set of leagues we know well. When nothing looks worth it, we don't publish — a quiet day is better than a forced pick.

Why we show losses

Our results page shows every pick we've ever published, including the ones that lost. Some betting sites only show winners. We don't. A record with losses is a real record. A record without them is a lie.

What we don't do

  • We don't claim to have a "system" that beats the bookmakers.
  • We don't sell "guaranteed wins" or promise any specific outcome.
  • We don't run fake countdown timers or invented scarcity.
  • We don't hide losing picks from the public record.

What we do

  • We research and pick carefully, and publish what we'd back ourselves.
  • We charge a simple subscription and we auto-renew you.
  • We treat our subscribers like adults.
  • We show you the good and the bad.
